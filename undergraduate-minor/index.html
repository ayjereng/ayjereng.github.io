Undergraduate programs

Undergraduate minor
in Demography

Build analytical skills. Understand a changing world.

From housing and healthcare to migration and the workforce, decisions depend on understanding people. Develop the tools to explore those connections.

Explore requirementsAsk an advisor

Why choose demography?

Bring a population perspective to your major. Study how migration, aging, climate change, and family life connect with the questions you already care about.

  • Analyze population change. Build a foundation in population analysis and statistical methods.
  • Connect data with social questions. Examine how demographic change relates to society and the economy.
  • Follow your interests. Choose electives that connect quantitative methods with social science.

Full program requirements

Planning questions

Can a course count toward both my major and minor?

One upper-division course may overlap. Discuss your proposed courses with your major advisor and demography advising before finalizing your plan.

When should I declare?

Declare by the last day of RRR week in the semester before your expected graduation term. Contact advising early to confirm the process for your timeline.

How should I plan around course availability?

The department lists DEMOG 110 and C126 for fall, and C175 for spring. Check current offerings and prerequisites before enrolling; required courses are generally offered once per academic year.

Forms & completion

Complete the minor paperwork

After you have finished or are enrolled in the remaining required courses, complete the L&S minor paperwork and submit it to Demography advising.

Open L&S formsEmail advising
  1. Download the “Completion of L&S Minor” form.Use the current version on the L&S Forms and Petitions page.
  2. Complete the Major/Minor Overlap Check.Obtain your major advisor’s signature. Only one upper-division course may overlap between the major and minor.
  3. Submit the supporting materials.Include an unofficial transcript and written approval for any course substitutions.

Substitutions and special plans

Request approval before using a substitute course. It must be upper division and at least 3 units; include the Academic Guide description, syllabus, and the requirement it would replace. Consult Demography before using study-abroad coursework. To cancel the minor, contact undergraduate advising.
